## 🎯 3. TOP 25 TOUGHEST SIH JURY QUESTIONS & WINNING ANSWERS

### Q1. Existing systems jaise IMD weather bulletins ya NDMA portal se AlertNex kaise alag hai?
**Answer:** IMD bulletins broad regional advisories dete hain (e.g. *"Heavy rainfall expected in Meghalaya"*), jisse local road aur village level par actionable clarity nahi milti. AlertNex 3 critical gaps solve karta hai:
1. **Hyper-local 30m Digital Elevation Model (DEM) resolution risk scoring** combining slope, soil moisture, and rainfall telemetry.
2. **Explainable AI (XAI)** jo commanders ko alert ka exact mathematical factor attribution batata hai.
3. **Topological Road Connectivity Solver** jo batata hai kaun sa gaon cut-off hua aur alternative emergency bypass route kaun sa open hai.

---

### Q2. Cloudburst ke dauran jab mobile cellular network down ho jaata hai, tab ground reports kaise transfer hongi?
**Answer:** AlertNex me **Offline-First IndexedDB Architecture** implement kiya gaya hai. Jab frontline field officers dead network zone me hote hain, app camera photo, GPS location aur hazard parameters ko device memory me securely store kar leti hai. Jaise hi phone network range ya satellite mesh terminal ke paas aata hai, background Service Worker automatically data server se sync kar deta hai.

---

### Q3. Aapka AI Model kaun sa use ho raha hai aur feature weights kaise calculate hote hain?
**Answer:** Hamara core AI engine multi-parametric weighted vulnerability matrix use karta hai calibrated against historical NER geological hazard patterns:
- **24h Cumulative Precipitation:** 30% weight
- **Soil Moisture Saturation (%):** 25% weight
- **DEM Slope Angle (Degrees):** 20% weight
- **Historical Landslide Frequency Index:** 15% weight
- **Verified Ground Field Reports:** 10% weight  
Risk Classes: 🟢 **Low (0–30%)**, 🟡 **Moderate (31–60%)**, 🟠 **High (61–80%)**, 🔴 **Critical (81–100%)**.

---

### Q4. Explainable AI (XAI) ka real-world disaster management me kya fayda hai?
**Answer:** Traditional black-box neural networks alert dete hain lekin decision justification nahi dete, jisse District Magistrates multimillion-rupee evacuation order issue karne me hesitate karte hain. AlertNex ka XAI engine har prediction ka dynamic factor attribution provide karta hai (e.g. *"88% Risk driven by 145mm rainfall + 82% soil moisture on a 38-degree slope"*). Isse administration confident, auditable aur fast decision le sakti hai.

---

### Q5. Topological Road Connectivity Graph Solver kaise kaam karta hai?
**Answer:** Hum Northeast ke road network ko mathematical Graph G = (V, E) ke roop me model karte hain jahan Vertices V settlements/junctions hain aur Edges E connecting highways hain. Agar koi road landslide se block hoti hai, hamara Dijkstra algorithm instantly lowest-risk emergency bypass detour compute karta hai aur nearest emergency hospital ka updated travel time calculate karta hai.

---

### Q6. Citizen reports me fake ya spam hazard reports ko kaise filter karte hain?
**Answer:** Hum 3-tier validation protocol use karte hain: (1) Spatial Clustering (500m radius multi-report validation); (2) Telemetry Cross-Verification; (3) Role-Based Authentication (SDRF/BRO officers get high trust score).

---

### Q7. Backend architecture aur database design kya hai?
**Answer:** Backend Python 3.12 FastAPI async framework par based hai jo high-concurrency requests handle karta hai. Database layer SQLAlchemy 2.0 ORM ke through PostGIS-enabled PostgreSQL (or embedded SQLite in prototype) use karta hai.

---

### Q8. 30-meter DEM (Digital Elevation Model) ka slope calculation kaise hota hai?
**Answer:** Hum SRTM 30m resolution elevation data ingest karte hain. 3x3 pixel window ke directional gradients dz/dx aur dz/dy se slope angle theta calculate hota hai. Northeast me >35 degree slopes high-risk category me aati hain.

---

### Q9. Kya ye system CWC, IMD, GSI aur BRO ke data ke saath integrate ho sakta hai?
**Answer:** Haan, AlertNex standard GeoJSON, REST Webhooks, aur CAP (Common Alerting Protocol - ITU X.1303) compliant alert payloads export karta hai jo NDMA aur state emergency command centres se direct integrate hota hai.

---

### Q10. Low-bandwidth rural internet par frontend ki performance kaisi rehti hai?
**Answer:** Frontend pure vanilla HTML5, optimized CSS3 tokens aur modular JavaScript me built hai without bulky framework bloat. Total bundle size < 250KB hai with sub-second load time on 2G/3G networks.

---

### Q11. Is system ka maintenance aur deployment cost kya hoga state government ke liye?
**Answer:** AlertNex completely open-source technologies (FastAPI, Leaflet, PostGIS, Linux) par built hai. State-wide cloud hosting serverless architecture par less than ₹20,000 - ₹30,000 per month me operate ho sakti hai.

---

### Q12. Security aur Emergency Dispatch authorization kaise ensure hoti hai?
**Answer:** System me Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) with JWT authentication implement kiya gaya hai. Siren broadcast aur emergency evacuation triggers keval authenticated District Disaster Commanders execute kar sakte hain.

---

### Q13. Prototype vs Production version me kya farq hai?
**Answer:** Prototype version calibrated synthetic telemetry data aur realistic NER terrain nodes use karta hai. Production roll-out me ye direct CWC automated rain gauges aur ISRO Bhuvan satellite telemetry feeds se connect hoga.

---

### Q14. Northeast India ke tribal communities tak alert kaise pahuchega?
**Answer:** Multi-channel alert dispatch: (1) Offline SMS Cell-Broadcast (basic 2G phones); (2) Local Community Siren Audio Triggers; (3) Village Disaster Management Committees (VDMC) WhatsApp / Telegram emergency bots.

---

### Q15. Landslide prediction me soil moisture data itna critical kyu hai?
**Answer:** Rainfall akele landslide nahi banata — jab rainwater soil pore water pressure ko increase karta hai aur cohesion loss hoti hai, tab slope fail hota hai. Soil moisture saturation slope failure limit ko accurate capture karti hai.

---

### Q16. Map view me kaun-kaun se interactive filters available hain?
**Answer:** GIS map me Risk Level filters (Low, Moderate, High, Critical), Telemetry Sensor Overlays, Road Network Layer, Village Vulnerability Markers aur Historical Slide hotspots available hain.

---

### Q17. System failure ya server crash ke time disaster protocol kya hai?
**Answer:** Multi-Region Edge CDN + Client-side Service Worker last-known risk state aur evacuation maps ko local cache me preserve rakhta hai even if backend is unreachable.

---

### Q18. Kya ye system earthquake-triggered landslides ko bhi factor karta hai?
**Answer:** Northeast Seismic Zone V me fall karta hai. Historical landslide index aur risk matrix me seismic fault proximity ko baseline hazard coefficient ke roop me factor kiya gaya hai.

---
